🔗 BLOCKCHAIN & NFT GUIDE

🪙 What is OKC?
OKC (Occupant Killer Coin) is the in-game currency you earn by destroying blocks, killing enemies, and completing waves. Every bullet that shatters terrain earns you +1 OKC. Save up OKC to buy weapons, skins, and premium boosts — or convert it to on-chain tokens later.
💎 What is POL?
POL (Polygon) is the real blockchain currency used for NFT minting and premium purchases. When you connect your MetaMask wallet, you can convert OKC to POL at a fixed rate, or spend POL directly on exclusive Veteran NFTs and weapon skins.
🎨 What are NFTs here?
Veteran NFTs are soulbound tokens that prove your combat record — kills, prestige level, and medals — forever on the Polygon blockchain. Weapons NFTs let you own unique gun skins that persist across devices. 10% of every POL purchase goes to the 🇺🇦 Ukrainian defense wallet.
🦊 How do I connect?
Click Connect Wallet above. You need the MetaMask browser extension (or any WalletConnect-compatible app). Make sure you're on the Polygon Mainnet. No wallet? No problem — you can still earn OKC and play everything for free.
🔄 Conversion Flow
1. Earn OKC in-game (bullets → blocks → OKC)
2. Connect wallet on Polygon
3. Convert OKC → POL (or OKC → Gold)
4. Mint Veteran NFT or buy Weapon Skin NFT
5. Trade on OpenSea or keep forever
