Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 June 2026

OccupantKiller ("the Game", "we", "us") is a browser-based game that runs almost entirely on your own device. This policy explains what little data is involved, where it lives, and the choices you have. We designed the Game to need as little of your information as possible.

Plain-English summary: the playable build collects no personal information and has no accounts by default. Your progress and settings are saved locally in your browser. Some optional online extras (off by default) need additional data only if you switch them on.

1. Information we do not collect

The standard playable build does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, address, or payment details, and does not run third-party advertising or behavioural tracking. We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not collect it.

2. Local browser storage (not cookies for tracking)

To make the Game playable, your browser stores a small amount of data on your device using localStorage / IndexedDB. This never leaves your machine unless you enable an optional online feature. It includes:

  • Game progress (current stage, unlocked weapons, score, settings).
  • Preferences (audio volume, control scheme, graphics options).
  • A locally generated, anonymous player id used only to label your own save.

You can clear all of it at any time by clearing site data for this domain in your browser settings. Doing so resets your local progress.

3. Optional online features (opt-in)

The Game can connect to optional services only if you choose to use them. When off, the Game runs fully offline. If you enable them, the minimum necessary data is sent:

  • Cloud save / leaderboards: your anonymous player id, scores and progress, so they can sync or rank.
  • In-game $OKC rewards: a wallet/identifier you provide, used only to attribute in-game token rewards. $OKC is an in-game utility/reward token and is not financial advice.
  • Soundtrack streaming: the Game may request a list of music tracks from our server to play in-game audio.

4. Third-party resources

  • Google Fonts may be loaded to render the interface; Google may receive your IP address as part of serving the font files. See Google's privacy policy.
  • The Game engine (Three.js) and other code run locally in your browser.

5. Children

OccupantKiller depicts armed conflict and is intended for a mature audience (16+/18+ where applicable). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Data security & retention

Local data stays on your device under your control. Where an optional online feature is used, data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and retained only as long as needed for that feature. You may request deletion of any optional online data tied to your identifier (see Contact).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data and to object to processing. Because the default build holds no personal data about you, most requests can be satisfied simply by clearing your browser storage. For optional online data, contact us.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the Game evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@photon-bounce.com

This document is provided as a general template for an informational browser game and is not legal advice. Please review it against your actual data practices and the laws that apply to you.
