BUILDING REFERENCE DOCUMENTATION
================================

Based on real-world research for OccupantKiller game building overhaul.

=== HOSTOMEL (Stage 0) ===
Reference: Antonov Airport (GML/UKKM)
- Control Tower: Tall narrow concrete, glass windows, antenna on top
- Hangars: Large arched metal buildings, 50m wide
- Administrative Building: 3-story Soviet office, rectangular
- An-225 Mriya: World's largest cargo aircraft, destroyed 2022
- Fuel Storage: Cylindrical white tanks, 10m tall
- Radar Dome: White sphere on 5m pedestal

Real features: Airport was first major battle of invasion (Feb 24, 2022)
Russian airborne assault repelled by Ukrainian National Guard.

=== KYIV (Stage 1) ===
Reference: Maidan Nezalezhnosti, Independence Monument
- Independence Monument: 62m tall white marble column, golden Berehynia statue
- Hotel Ukraina: Stalinist skyscraper, 198m tall, 28 floors
- Khreshchatyk: Main street, Stalinist Empire style, chestnut trees
- St. Sophia Cathedral: Byzantine, 11th century, golden domes, UNESCO
- Golden Gate: Medieval fortress gate, reconstructed 1982
- Kyiv-Pechersk Lavra: Monastery, golden domes, caves below
- Central Post Office: 1952-1957, Soviet neoclassical

=== MOSCOW (Stage 2/5/19) ===
Reference: Kremlin, Red Square, St. Basil's
- St. Basil's Cathedral: 1555-1561, colorful onion domes, 9 towers
- Kremlin Wall: 2.2km red brick, 20 towers, 15th-17th century
- Spasskaya Tower: 71m tall, red star, clock
- Lenin Mausoleum: Red/black granite pyramid, 1924
- Seven Sisters: Stalinist skyscrapers, 1947-1953
- Bolshoi Theater: Classical columns, 1825
- Christ the Savior: 1883, white with golden domes, 103m tall
- Ostankino Tower: 540m tall, lattice structure, 1967

=== MARIUPOL (Stage 3) ===
Reference: Azovstal, Port of Mariupol
- Azovstal: 10km² steel plant, blast furnaces, coke ovens, underground tunnels
- Ilyich Iron & Steel: Second plant, ~40,000 workers
- Port: Cranes, shipping containers, grain elevators
- Drama Theater: 1960, large modernist building (destroyed 2022)
- Lighthouse: White tower, Sea of Azov coast

Real features: City of 450,000, almost completely destroyed in siege.
Azovstal became symbol of resistance (April-May 2022).

=== BAKHMUT (Stage 4) ===
Reference: Bakhmut Fortress, Salt Mines
- Bakhmut Fortress: 17th century, stone walls, ramparts
- St. Nicolas Church: 1797, white with blue domes
- St. John Baptist: 1861, belfry prominent
- Railway College: 120+ years old, brick, 3-story
- Salt Mine Entrance: White tunnel entrance, industrial buildings
- City Center: 4-10 story panel apartment blocks
- Bakhmutka River: Runs through city, 2 bridges

Real features: 10-month battle (Aug 2022-May 2023), city completely destroyed.
100,000 Russian casualties to capture.

=== CHORNOBYL (Stage 7) ===
Reference: Exclusion Zone, Pripyat
- Reactor 4 Sarcophagus: New Safe Confinement, 110m tall arch, 2016
- Duga Radar: 150m tall steel antenna array, "Russian Woodpecker"
- Abandoned Pripyat: 16-story panel apartments, crumbling
- Pripyat School: 3-story, Soviet murals, abandoned 1986
- Pripyat Hotel: 10-story, Polissya Hotel
- Amusement Park: Ferris wheel (never opened), bumper cars
- Swimming Pool: Azure Pool, rectangular, diving board
- Red Forest: Scots pine, dead from radiation, reddish-brown

Real features: 1986 disaster, 30km exclusion zone, Pripyat evacuated in hours.

=== CRIMEA BRIDGE (Stage 8) ===
Reference: Kerch Strait Bridge
- Bridge: 19km long, 4-lane highway + rail, opened 2018
- Tuzla Island: Small sandy island in Kerch Strait
- Kerch Fortress: Ancient stone walls, 15th century
- Port of Kerch: Ferry terminal, small harbor

=== SEVASTOPOL (Stage 11) ===
Reference: Naval Base, Black Sea Fleet
- Grafskaya Wharf: Stone pier with classical columns
- Balaklava Submarine Base: Underground tunnels, dry dock
- St. Vladimir Cathedral: White with golden domes, 19th century
- Panorama Museum: Circular building, Defense of Sevastopol 1854-55
- Coastal Fortresses: Stone walls with cannons

=== DONBAS (Stage 12) ===
Reference: Mining Region
- Mine Headframe: Tall steel tower with winding wheels
- Slag Heap (Terrikon): Artificial cone-shaped hill, 100-200m tall
- Coal Processing Plant: Conveyor belts, sorting equipment
- Worker Housing: 2-story brick with small balconies
- Culture Palace: Soviet-era community center

=== SNAKE ISLAND (Stage 14) ===
Reference: Zmiinyi Island
- Lighthouse: White tower, 40m tall, 1842
- Border Post: Small building with Ukrainian flag
- Defensive Positions: Trenches, sandbags, concrete bunkers
- Helipad: Concrete landing pad, small
- Radio Tower: Tall metal antenna

Real features: Famous "Russian warship, go f*** yourself" incident (Feb 2022).
Island traded hands multiple times.

=== SAKY (Stage 15) ===
Reference: Airbase, Crimea
- Main Runway: 3km long, 60m wide, concrete
- Hangars: Large arched metal buildings
- Control Tower: Glass windows, radar on top
- Fuel Depot: Cylindrical tanks, underground pipelines
- Barracks: 2-story long buildings, Soviet standard

=== VUHLEDAR (Stage 16) ===
Reference: Coal Mining Town
- Mine Headframe: Tall steel structure, winding gear
- Coal Slag Heap: Flat-topped conical hill, terraced
- Coal Preparation Plant: Industrial buildings, conveyors
- Thermal Power Plant: Tall chimneys, cooling towers
- Worker Housing: 5-story panel blocks, Soviet standard

=== ANTONOV BRIDGE (Stage 17) ===
Reference: Dnipro Crossing, Kherson
- Bridge: Road/rail bridge, 1.4km long, 30 spans
- Bridge Towers: Concrete pylons, 20m tall
- Kherson Side: Fortifications, trenches, bunkers
- Russian Side: Checkpoint, barriers, guardhouse
- Pontoon Bridge: Floating sections, military construction

=== OIL REFINERY (Stage 18) ===
Reference: Petrochemical Complex
- Distillation Tower: 50m tall, pipes and platforms
- Storage Tanks: 10+ large cylindrical tanks, 20m tall
- Flare Stack: Tall pipe with constant flame
- Pipeline Manifold: Network of pipes connecting units
- Catalytic Cracker: Complex industrial structure
- Cooling Tower: Hyperbolic concrete, 40m tall

=== SIEGE OF MOSCOW (Stage 19) ===
Reference: Final Assault Scenario
- Kremlin Wall: Red brick, defensive positions
- Red Square: Large plaza, cobblestones
- St. Basil's: Colorful domes, damaged
- Barricades: Concrete blocks, sandbags, burning vehicles
- Government Buildings: White facades, bullet holes
- Metro Entrances: M signs, used as bomb shelters
